Vegan food for everyone who’s hungry.

Bostan is a plant-based kitchen in Cluj-Napoca. We cook breakfast, lunch and dinner every day, at fair prices and in portions that keep you full until the next meal.

Why people keep coming back

Bostan is another Romanian word for pumpkin, and like a pumpkin we’re big, cheerful and hard to dislike. You don’t have to be vegan to eat here. Most of our regulars aren’t.

Only plants, no lectures

Everything we cook is vegan, down to the wine and the sugar. Nobody will ask you to be.

Prices that fit a student budget

Soup and a main for 35 lei on weekdays. Show a student card and you get 10% off the rest of the menu.

Allergens on every dish

Each plate is marked for gluten, nuts, soy and sesame, and our staff can check anything else with the kitchen.

Room for everyone

A step-free entrance, high chairs, half portions for children and water bowls for dogs on the terrace.

Good to know

The questions we hear most often at the counter.

Yes. There is no meat, fish, dairy, egg or honey in anything we serve. We also choose wine and beer that are made without animal products in the filtering.

Many dishes are gluten-free or can be made so, and every dish on the menu lists its allergens. Our kitchen also handles wheat and nuts, so if you have a severe allergy, tell us when you order and we’ll go through the options with you.
