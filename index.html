Pull up a chair. It’s all plants.

Garden Table is a vegan kitchen in Cluj-Napoca. We cook breakfast, lunch and dinner every day, with fair prices, generous portions and a seat for anyone who’s hungry.

Open every day from 8:00 to 22:00 on Strada Moților 41

Why people keep coming back

We called it Garden Table because that’s all we wanted to be: food that grows in a garden, served at a table with room for everyone. You don’t have to be vegan to eat here. Most of our regulars aren’t.

Only plants, no lectures

Everything we cook is vegan, down to the bread and the sugar. Nobody will ask you to be.

Fair prices, every day

Soup and a main for 35 lei on weekdays, and 10% off the menu with a student card.

Allergens on every dish

Each plate lists gluten, nuts, soy, sesame and celery, and many dishes come gluten-free or can be made so.

Room for everyone

A step-free entrance, high chairs, half portions for children and water bowls for dogs on the terrace.

Good to know

Every dish on the menu lists its allergens, and many are gluten-free or can be made so. Several desserts are made with nuts, and our kitchen also handles wheat, so if you have a severe allergy, tell us when you order and we’ll go through the options with you.
